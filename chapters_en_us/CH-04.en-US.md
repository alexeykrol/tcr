# CH-04 -- WAYS TO INCREASE INCOME

### Ways to Increase Income
Let's summarize our analysis of the nature of income.

Representatives of the lower castes work, but all their efforts go only toward maintaining a bleak existence. Their opportunities do not grow.

Representatives of the middle castes maintain a high standard of living, but the growth of income and opportunities is limited by the time of their individual labor.

Representatives of the upper castes have an exceptionally high standard of living and unlimited resources for growth, and the rate at which their opportunities grow also increases.

The key lesson you can draw from this is: if you want to truly achieve something in life, you need to reach the upper castes. Only there will the level of your opportunities increase and stop depending on your personal time. Usually, once a person realizes the need for growth, they start looking for sources of quick income. Alas, if they are in a slave caste, their horizon is extremely limited. They begin to go through accelerated-earning methods that are accessible to their understanding. How can I change my financial situation as quickly as possible, they ask themselves, and they find a whole range of answers:
- find a treasure, a wallet, or some other ownerless valuable;
- steal, rob, or get money through deception;
- find someone who will support them;
- get a second or third job;
- become a successful blogger;
- borrow money;
- reduce consumption to a minimum.

But all these sources have the same drawbacks. One of the downsides of all the options listed above is that the financial resource is exhaustible. Money comes to you while you are taking purposeful actions. It does not matter whether you are working, robbing, or providing someone with a comfortable life. The analogy with a shark suggests itself: it breathes while it swims. It stops--that's it, oxygen stops flowing. And if we are talking about illegal ways of earning money, there are other downsides too--for example, the constant risk of ending up in prison and smoothly moving into the caste of Criminals.

In any case, if you want a steady movement upward along the caste pyramid, one obvious approach is to build your own business. Create a flow of money that is not tied to your moment-to-moment actions. A business implies that you stop trading your time and competencies and instead make it so other people work for you.

By the way, many bloggers have realized this simple truth as well. Several years ago they started launching their own clothing brands, cosmetics, and online courses en masse. Why? Because constantly trading on your face is, in essence, not much different from selling your time and skills. Take a break, or miss the audience's expectations a few times, and followers leave the blog, advertisers do not renew contracts, the media ignores you. But a business with something under it besides regular online appearances provides some stability and builds financial reserves.

Building your own thing is hard. It is like creating an entire world. But it is worth it. Nothing else can replace the feeling of watching your own creation that can feed and protect you. At the same time, within [Theory of Castes and Roles](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07PDHZRCC) we understand the expression "your own thing" somewhat more broadly than is customary. This is not only about business as such--small, medium, or large--but about organizing production or other activity in such a way that it multiplies capital without your constant involvement.

So what could it be?

1) Creating works of art or science that can be republished and reproduced: books, music, films, inventions, discoveries, methodologies. This is the path of the artist and the scholar. Creativity brings pleasure; you can plunge into its depths, soar into higher spheres... and, as ordinary people believe, not engage in direct commerce, not fight for assets, not take part in intrigues. It is enough to create something brilliant, many think--something that will capture people's hearts and can be reproduced and licensed. This path attracts many people because it promises fame and money. However, competition is very high. And the realities of the modern world hint, not very transparently, that it is not enough to create something brilliant--you must be able to sell it. And so the writer is already pushing their personal brand in the media, running social media, learning the basics of social media marketing. And the scientist personally presents their developments to investors. But the fact remains: in a world where content rules everything, making content production the foundation of your own thing is a working strategy.

2) Monetizing the right to a resource--land, real estate, equipment, broadcast frequencies--anything that can bring rent. To follow this path, you need to build, buy, inherit, or take something that will later bring stable income. A very attractive path, but one that is open to few. To build such a thing, you need access to big money and connections.

3) Business as a system whose purpose is to produce, sell, and provide services. And you, as the owner, have a right to part of its profit. This is the path of the Businessman. Many try to follow it, because, by and large, it does not require as much as it may seem. Above all, insight to understand what people need here and now and what they are willing to pay for. And energy to set up a system that will satisfy those needs.

The five percent who belong to the upper castes have their own thing--either one of the three options listed above, or several. Therefore, if you want to find yourself at the top level of the hierarchy, your main task is to acquire your own thing. And it does not matter whether you create it yourself, buy it, or inherit it. The main thing is that it works for you. If you do not want to have your own thing, sooner or later you will end up in the lower castes, even if you had a lot of money for a long time.

This applies first and foremost to creative personalities, athletes, and lucky traders. They earn huge money, but if they are not smart enough at the right moment to buy a stake in some business, their well-being is doomed. They will have nothing to pass on to their children. A business can be bequeathed; personal mastery cannot--unless it is inherited genetically.

Thus, the strategy presented here is intended first and foremost for those who understand and accept the postulate that the ultimate goal in social-financial terms is to have your own thing, or a stake in it, in order to receive passive or active income. If you achieve this goal, it, in turn, will make it possible to bring to life a huge number of ambitious plans for which you currently have neither money nor time. Note that the thesis about the need to have a business--or at least passive income--is relevant for salaried high-level executives and for Siloviki as well. From a financial standpoint, the outcome of their career is an option in a successful company, as well as a substantial pension from that same company.

Here I want to make a note. Many people, especially young people, say: I don't need this; I'm a professional; I'm doing fine as it is. They think so because they are young and do not feel the presence nearby of the most patient and experienced opponent of all. Its name is time.

At the same time, I want to emphasize: I am not claiming that creating your own thing or participating in someone else's business is the only and the most important goal of life. You may be far more attracted by the idea of travel or fighting for the environment. Great. But I remind you that life in human society entails daily expenses for food, housing, transportation, clothing. That is the reality of this world. If you have your own thing that ideally does not require your full involvement, that makes you the master of your own time. And that means you can use it to realize whatever you want--including travel and fighting for the environment. But if you are not the master of your time, you are simply a slave to necessity. Your mind may be bursting at the seams with brilliant ideas, but you will have no chance to realize them.

Therefore, before you start yelling about your desires and ideas, become free and provide yourself with opportunities. This book is devoted to how to do that. So, we have formulated an intermediate goal: your own thing, and in the longer term, passive income. Achieving this goal will move you into a caste with greater opportunities and will allow you to realize the most ambitious plans. That is why creating your own thing is an intermediate goal.

Once the point on the map where we must be has been defined, and the point where we are now is known, it is time to choose:
- a growth strategy;
- the path that will lead us to the goal;
- the means we will use;
- the necessary resources.

### Growth Strategies in the World of Castes
Many of those who become familiar with my theory fall under the power of an illusion. They incorrectly determine their caste, confusing it with a job title and profession. It is important to soberly evaluate the place you occupy in the social hierarchy so as not to take actions that can lead you somewhere else. To simplify the task, I will give you a guideline. If your income exceeds your expense level by no more than 50 percent and you have no ability to build reserves, then you belong to a slave caste: Officials, Slaves, Gendarmes. And it does not matter whether you work for yourself or for an employer. Your profession, position, and education also do not matter at all. You are a Slave because you depend on factors you cannot control. The overwhelming majority of people on planet Earth belong to the caste of Slaves, although the quantitative ratio of representatives of different castes varies from country to country.

The starting point is the caste of Slaves. What the final point will be depends on your desire and your faith in your own strength. But once you define the starting and ending points of the path, a vector of movement appears, and that means it is time to choose a growth strategy. In the world of castes, there are not that many correct, upward strategies.

### Growth Strategies of the Lower and Middle Castes
The end goal of any strategy for the lower castes is to get into the caste of Businessmen or Top Managers, or, at worst, into Rentiers or Professionals. These are all realistic goals, dictated by the logic of the hierarchy of human society.

Strategy 1: Slave -> Professional -> Free Agent and Entrepreneur -> Businessman.
Strategy 2: Slave -> Official -> Top Manager.
Strategy 3: Slave -> Free Agent and Entrepreneur -> Businessman.
Strategy 4: Official -> Free Agent and Entrepreneur -> Businessman.
Strategy 5: Professional -> Official -> Top Manager.
Strategy 6: Professional -> Free Agent and Entrepreneur -> Businessman.
Strategy 7: Entrepreneur -> Official -> Top Manager.

### Growth Strategies of the Upper Castes
The end goal of any strategy for the upper castes is to get into the caste of Oligarchs/Magnates or Rulers.

Strategy 1: Top Manager -> Businessman.
Strategy 2: Gendarme -> Official -> Silovik.
Strategy 3: Silovik -> Businessman -> Oligarch/Magnate.
Strategy 4: Top Manager -> Silovik.
Strategy 5: Top Manager -> Ruler.
Strategy 6: Oligarch/Magnate -> Businessman -> Ruler.
Strategy 7: Silovik -> Ruler.

### The Main Factor of Successful Growth
In essence, the key goal for every active member of society should be movement upward along the hierarchical pyramid. For representatives of the lower castes, it is vitally important to leave the caste of Slaves once and for all in order to move at least into the caste of Rentiers, while remaining in the castes of Professionals and Entrepreneurs. The most reasonable strategy in their case is the creation or purchase of passive income. It will generate funds, freeing up room for maneuver--making it possible to set up a system of constant cash inflow without full involvement in the process.

For representatives of the upper castes, growth will be expressed in the expansion of their field of influence. And here the main instrument is political intrigue, creating coalitions, joining strong groups, and so on.

If we talk about the key factor of any growth, regardless of caste, it lies far beyond professional skills. No matter how first-class a specialist you are (a welder, a baker, a journalist, a teacher, a driver, a sales manager, a department head), it will not ensure your advancement in the hierarchy. A good baker will stand at the stove their whole life, and a good journalist will churn out articles. In a certain sense, being absorbed in your craft blocks further growth and prevents you from breaking out beyond the limits of your caste. Because growth requires effort not in a narrow professional field, but, if you like, in an interdisciplinary one.

If you intend to move upward, it is important to invest in interaction with other people: in developing relationships with those who, at the right moment, will facilitate your career rise; in the ability to make the right impression; in the willingness to take on as much responsibility as possible (for the financial plan, for the efficiency of a unit, for other employees). Being an effective communicator, confident in your abilities--that is the most correct path upward. Only it will lead you to stable growth.

Remember: there are many professionals, but only a few become managers, top executives, and even more so large businessmen. And it is not only about their intelligence or aggressiveness. The issue, as was said above, lies in the nature of communication with those around them. Or, in other words, in the social role that one or another person plays. That is what we will talk about next.
