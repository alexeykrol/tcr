# PREFACE TO THE NEW EDITION

You are holding the second edition of the book [Theory of Castes and Roles](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07PDHZRCC), which first appeared in print in 2017. At the same time, its text became available to the public much earlier--back in 2013. I posted it on my Facebook account, where I had only 300 followers. Around the same time, my wife and I left for the United States to launch our startup in Silicon Valley.

Since the first publication on the Internet, I have received more than ten thousand letters with readers' feedback. People of all kinds from more than seventy countries told me that thanks to [Theory of Castes and Roles](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07PDHZRCC) they gained a crystal-clear understanding of the unspoken structure of the modern world. They received an honest map of reality, on which you can clearly see where you yourself are, what you've managed to achieve, what prospects you have, and whether you have any at all.

Today, watching the lives of my regular subscribers, I can in good conscience recommend [Theory of Castes and Roles](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07PDHZRCC) to anyone who, regardless of age and country of residence, feels they've hit a dead end. By a dead end, I mean a state of affairs when:

- your income is not growing by at least 15% year over year;
- your social role is not changing;
- you feel discomfort about it, and sometimes irritation and even rage.

I know what I'm talking about, because I once found myself in a similar situation. Remember the Strugatskys' *Monday Begins on Saturday*? "And they adopted the working hypothesis that happiness lies in the continuous pursuit of the unknown and that the meaning of life lies in the same." For a long time I lived as if that hypothesis were a proven theorem--until I realized it was a beautiful but false statement.

The realization came many years ago, when I was sitting alone in my director's office after I decided to leave the company I had built. I sat there asking myself: why does my career look like a roller coaster, up and down, earned-lost, achieved-rolled back? I gained easily and just as easily lost everything. Money, connections, property--everything flowed through me; nothing remained; only the years flew by. I wanted to find the hole my life was draining into.

The search took nine years. I tested newer and newer hypotheses, watched myself and the world's reactions to this or that action, experimented. And my life gradually changed. I found answers to the questions that had been tormenting me and began to follow my own advice. Now I have more than a hundred thousand followers on social media, and about that many receive my weekly newsletter. I am fully focused on writing and communicating with readers, which allows me to feel completely self-realized.

As for my business, it is built entirely on a subscription model. The clients are my readers, living all over the world. I engage in meticulous research into the patterns of failure and growth in order to share insights that help people find a way out of life's dead end. I make it possible to see a door that, for some reason, went unnoticed. To see it and feel a sharp desire to open it.

I not only managed to turn the key and step over the threshold, but also traveled a gigantic path from "to hell with it all" to "this is what I wanted." I was 50 when I moved to the United States; now I'm 58. My wife and I built a business from scratch in a foreign country--without a team or investments. For me, it's obvious: growth, financial freedom, self-realization--these are not miracles or luck. They are technology, discipline of thought, and an adequate picture of the world. And also knowledge of the rules of the Game that each of us enters by virtue of being born.

Most people discover them in bits and pieces: in the course of a chance conversation or on the basis of their own, often painful, experience. Consider [Theory of Castes and Roles](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B07PDHZRCC) something like a manual for the game called "Human Life." It provides the most realistic possible map of the playing field, a complete list of participants and their capabilities, and the mechanisms that will allow you to move in any direction. This manual will help you correctly define your mission, assess the resources you have, and set a goal. Any game is good in that it has no one-hundred-percent limitations. If you know how, you can reach any level. The main thing is to act with your eyes wide open.
