# CH-12 -- SWOT ANALYSIS OF DIFFERENT CASTES

SWOT Analysis of Different Castes
RULERS
Threats: Coup aimed at seizing power. Assassination attempt. Revolt or betrayal by the entourage from lower castes.
Weaknesses: Dependence on servants and on subordinates from lower castes. Difficulty obtaining objective information. The need to be tense all the time. The need to manage the entourage and subordinates by the principle "divide and rule." The need to live by the very harsh codes of their caste. Possibility of execution for a serious violation of the rules. Distrust from the lower castes.
Opportunities: The right to establish or abolish rules and rights. Unlimited power. No accountability to anyone except oneself. Possession of everything, except for physical (disability) and resource (a resource-poor state) limitations. Also limitations connected with the likelihood of social explosion and damage to one's property.
Strengths: The highest concentration of power, resources, wealth, and quality of life for oneself and one's clients. Maximum property. Maximum political, security-force, and financial leverage. The ability not to comply with official laws. Stability (if there is no war or revolution). Social protection.
PRIESTS
Threats: Failing to please the Ruler. Internal coup aimed at seizing power. Assassination attempt.
Weaknesses: Lack of real coercive-force and significant financial resources. Limited ability to conduct special operations and actions. The need to maintain a public image. Limited by dogma. The need to maintain loyalty to the Ruler. Limited ability to intervene in state affairs. Inability to become an official ruler. To get into the caste, you must be someone's relative or, from early childhood, renounce yourself and devote yourself to service.
Opportunities: Lobbying and influence on the Ruler. Broad intermediary opportunities. The right to establish or abolish rules and rights within one's caste. Unlimited power within one's caste. No accountability to anyone except oneself, within one's caste. The ability not to comply with official laws.
Strengths: Control over loyal believers. The ability to confirm or revoke the legitimacy of power. The ability to consolidate large masses of people. The highest concentration of power, resources, wealth within the caste, quality of life for oneself and one's clients. Maximum property within the caste. Stability (if there is no war or revolution). Social protection.
SILOVIKS
Threats: Failing to please the Ruler. Internal coup aimed at seizing power. Assassination attempt. Military and security risks.
Weaknesses: Unquestioning obedience to the Rulers. The need to constantly prove absolute loyalty. The obligation to observe the codes of Rulers and warriors. For a serious violation of the rules, death or a fall into the caste of Slaves awaits him. To get into the caste, you must either be someone's relative or, from early childhood, renounce yourself and go serve. Distrust from the lower castes.
Opportunities: Become a Ruler. Lobbying and influence on the Ruler. Broad intermediary opportunities. The right to establish or abolish rules and rights within one's caste. Unlimited power within one's caste. No accountability to anyone except oneself, within one's caste. The ability not to comply with official laws.
Strengths: Control over loyal subordinates. Carrying out military and punitive functions. The ability to take power or support the Ruler. The ability to unite large masses of people. The highest concentration of power, resources, wealth within the caste, quality of life for oneself and one's clients. Maximum property within the caste. Stability (if there is no war or revolution). Social protection.
OLIGARCHS AND MAGNATES
Threats: Failing to please the Ruler, a Silovik, a Priest. Risk of being removed from all positions and targeted bankruptcy by the Ruler and Siloviks. Risk of going to prison for breaking the rules. Assassination attempt. Military and security risks.
Weaknesses: In reality, not that much belongs to you, since you are a banker and a moneybag for the Ruler and Siloviks. You are in a position; you were chosen and put there. You must carry out all orders. You must pay taxes and various tributes to Rulers, Siloviks, Criminals. Carry out the will of the Ruler, the Silovik, and sometimes even the Priest, if the Priest enjoys the patronage of the Ruler or the Silovik. You must bear full responsibility for everything. You must comply with all official laws, as well as the shadow codes of the Rulers, Siloviks, and Criminals. You are forced to break the law otherwise. You are always on a hook with the authorities, and if anything happens, the Rulers will deal with you brutally. You are always on a hook with the Siloviks and Criminals. Despite any financial difficulties, fees and taxes--white and shadow--are always paid first and foremost. It is impossible to stop paying taxes--you can only leave the caste. To stop being a member of the caste, you have to spend money and nerves, but ten times more than when entering the caste (this is leaving "the good way"). Leaving "the bad way" is prison, economic slavery, death, a fall into the lower castes.
Opportunities: Become a Ruler. Lobbying and influence on the Ruler. Broad intermediary opportunities. The right to establish or abolish rules and rights within one's empire. Unlimited power within one's caste. No accountability to anyone except the Ruler and oneself within one's caste. The ability not to comply with official laws.
Strengths: Having an active income system (a business). Money, well-being, a certain freedom, greater than that of Rulers, Siloviks, and Priests. In principle you do what you want; you control the situation in most cases. High importance. Significant financial security, high quality of life. The highest concentration of power, resources, wealth within the caste, quality of life for oneself and one's clients. Maximum formal property. Stability (if there is no war or revolution). Social protection.
BUSINESSMEN
Threats: Failing to please the Ruler, a Silovik, an Oligarch. Risk of targeted bankruptcy by the Ruler, Siloviks, Oligarchs. Risk of going to prison for breaking the rules. Assassination attempt. Risk of a business takeover. Risk of kidnapping family members. Risk of crossing an Oligarch or Magnate. Risk of bankruptcy from competitors and bad market conditions.
Weaknesses: You must not stick your neck out; you must not be too visible. Observe political correctness. You must pay taxes and various tributes to Rulers, Siloviks, Criminals. Carry out the will of the Ruler, the Silovik, and sometimes even the Priest, if the Priest enjoys the patronage of the Ruler or the Silovik. You must bear full responsibility for everything. You must comply with all official laws, as well as the shadow codes of the Rulers, Siloviks, and Criminals. You are forced to break the law. You are always on a hook with the authorities, and if anything happens, the Rulers will deal with you brutally. You are always on a hook with the Siloviks and Criminals. Despite any financial difficulties, fees and taxes--white and shadow--are always paid first and foremost. It is impossible to stop paying taxes--you can only leave the caste.
Opportunities: Become an Oligarch. Lobbying and influence on an Oligarch. Business opportunities dependent on permission from Rulers, Siloviks, Oligarchs. Broad intermediary opportunities. Unlimited power within your company if the company is private.
Strengths: Greater control over your fate than an Oligarch, a Ruler, a Silovik, a Priest. A greater degree of independence. Money, well-being, a certain freedom, greater than that of Rulers, Siloviks, and Oligarchs. In principle you do what you want; you control the situation in most cases. High importance, but less than that of Oligarchs. Significant financial security, high quality of life. The highest concentration of power, resources, wealth within the company, quality of life for oneself and one's clients. Significant property. Stability (if there is no war, revolution, crises, or competitors' schemes). Social protection.
TOP MANAGERS
Threats: Causing dissatisfaction in a Ruler or someone from his entourage. Causing dissatisfaction in an Oligarch. Causing dissatisfaction in the Siloviks. Crossing the lobbyists of opposing clans. Getting fired and falling out of the lineup (the talent pool). Falling under serious criminal and other liability. High probability of retiring from the peak of one's career.
Weaknesses: Practically cannot have their own opinion. In essence, a supervisor over the key assets of Rulers, Siloviks, and Oligarchs. Responsible for everything. Constantly tense from under-the-table games. If something goes wrong--resignation. You need to be very sensitive to your owners. You also need to manage the workforce, identify spies, be vigilant against personnel reshuffles from above. Be vigilant toward young competitors from below.
Opportunities: Become a Ruler or an Oligarch.
Strengths: Limited power, status, importance, significant social and financial benefits. Stability (if there is no war, revolution, crises, or competitors' schemes). Social protection while you are in the position and in the lineup. There is a chance that a tiny piece of property will fall to you from the upper castes. Shielded from the problems of the lower world.
RENTIERS
Threats: Losing savings in the event of a crisis, revolution, or war. Being deceived.
Weaknesses: Outside real business. Cannot influence anything. Limited funds for large projects. You have to constantly monitor the income source. You have to manage the budget. Do not argue with the authorities. Do not cross anyone. Protect property.
Opportunities: Efficiently manage property and income. Creatively self-realize within financial capabilities. The possibility to live as you like, calmly. Acquire various nice things and satisfy your own whims.
Strengths: Having a passive income system. High personal freedom. High quality of life, independence. The possibility of unlimited creative self-realization, time for yourself and loved ones, travel.
ENTREPRENEURS
Threats: Losing your business or your health due to unlawful actions, due to pressure from big business. In the end, becoming a Slave.
Weaknesses: Very low stability and business income. The need to be involved in the business all the time. Inability to build a systemic business. Little time for yourself and family. Hard. You are responsible for everything. The need to constantly work yourself so the business stays afloat. Very average stability and financial resilience. Growth can be limited by structured and mature markets and competitors.
Opportunities: Grow the business. Become a Businessman. Become an Official. Become a Professional.
Strengths: High flexibility and adaptability. Freedom of decision-making. High independence. Growth prospects. Average stability, average well-being. Everything depends on the person. Self-respect.
PROFESSIONALS
Threats: Losing qualification and reputation. Losing motivation and drinking yourself to death.
Weaknesses: You must pay taxes and pay for maintaining and achieving professionalism. To become a professional and earn authority, you need a lot of time, and sometimes you also have to bend to other professionals. To enter the caste, you need to invest money, nerves, and time. Do not conflict with anyone; keep professional neutrality, but work for the one who pays. You must constantly study and remain competitive. If you seriously violate the rules, nothing special, because the codes of this caste are very vague. But you can be fired. You do not do what you want, but what you are told.
Opportunities: Move into the castes of Entrepreneurs, Businessmen, and Officials.
Strengths: Money, well-being, a certain freedom, greater than that of rulers and warriors, but less than that of merchants. Constant demand on the labor market. Respect of the professional community. In equal positions (in the general case) are rulers, warriors, merchants, and criminals, because it is hard (though possible) to force a highly qualified person to work creatively and with full effort. You must be responsible only for the task assigned. You must follow only official laws. Leaving the caste--no problem. Easier to move abroad. Relatively easy to always find work if your profession is one of those that are in constant demand. You choose whom to provide services to. At a certain stage there is an opportunity to move into Entrepreneurs, Businessmen, Officials, and this by free choice. You can simultaneously be a member of the castes of Entrepreneurs and Businessmen. Very high career flexibility.
OFFICIALS
Threats: Risk of causing dissatisfaction in a Higher Manager or someone from his entourage. Getting fired and falling out of the lineup (the talent pool). High probability of retiring from the peak of one's career. Being taken down by internal competitors.
Weaknesses: Almost everything needs to be coordinated. Initiative is allowed, but without feats and without risks. In essence, a supervisor over a direction of key assets of Rulers, Siloviks, and Oligarchs. Responsible for your area. Constantly tense from under-the-table games. If something goes wrong--fired or demoted. You need to be very sensitive to your bosses and their entourage. You also need to manage the workforce, identify spies, be vigilant against personnel reshuffles from above. Be vigilant toward young competitors from below.
Opportunities: Become a Higher Manager.
Strengths: Minimal power in your zone of responsibility, small status, some importance, average social and financial benefits. Limited stability while you are in position (if there is no war, revolution, crises, or competitors' schemes). Social protection while you are in the position and in the lineup. Shielded from the problems of the lower world. In essence--a slave.
GENDARMES, POLICE, ARMY AND NAVY OFFICERS
Threats: Falling out of the caste of Gendarmes into the caste of Slaves or Dependents. Being injured or killed. Becoming scum.
Weaknesses: Total subordination to superiors and rules. A beastly job with moral violations. Destructively affects the personality. Develops stupidity and kills initiative. Instills suspicion. Creates a bad character. Not high financial and social stability. Not always respected in society.
Opportunities: Become an Official. You can partially not comply with the laws.
Strengths: If you serve honestly, you get a certain satisfaction. You bring benefit. You revel in power. Relative stability. Career opportunities.
SLAVES
Threats: Being fired, being oppressed by an employer. Falling into the caste of Untouchables.
Weaknesses: Very low resilience and independence. Strong dependence on employer, market, country, place of residence. Poverty. Constant problem of how to live, eat, dress, get treated, educate children, bury elders, do repairs. Need for constant loans. Not master of yourself and your time. Hard to get an education. Hard to give an education. Forced to work all the time to feed yourself. If you lose your job, you instantly end up in the caste of Untouchables. The boss is God and Tsar. No will of your own. They demand a lot. A certain kind of lack of rights (if you are not protected by a union). Hard to go beyond your assigned patch. Little development.
Opportunities: Become a professional, entrepreneur, official.
Strengths: Not responsible for anything. Less stress. Workday is over--order. If a state or large enterprise--social benefits and stability. Opportunities for advancement to the level of Official. Opportunity to learn in corporate trainings if the enterprise is decent. A relatively calm life: don't think about anything, work, drink vodka, and have kids--a serf-slave, in a word.
DEPENDENTS
Threats: Loss of a breadwinner or income source--moving into the caste of Untouchables.
Weaknesses: Dependent. Few opportunities for development. High risks connected with health. Harder to get an education.
Opportunities: Go work and study.
Strengths: No need to strain. Do what you want within the bounds of financial help or your health condition.
UNTOUCHABLES
Threats: Death from disease or injury.
Weaknesses: High vulnerability, risk of disease, injury, poisoning, freezing, and so on. Poor attitude from society.
Opportunities: Look for work. Commit crimes.
Strengths: A peculiar freedom from society and its conventions.
CRIMINALS
Threats: Imprisonment and death.
Weaknesses: Life as an outcast. Constantly have to be cautious. One-hundred-percent probability of ending up in prison with all the consequences.
Opportunities: Quit, go study and work.
Strengths: Freedom from society and its conventions. Everything depends on the person. Self-respect.
