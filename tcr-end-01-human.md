**THE NATURE OF INCOME IN DIFFERENT CASTES**

The castes can be divided into three main categories: high, middle and low. They differ not only by income level, but also by the nature of the income itself.

**INCOME IN THE LOWER CASTES**

The lower castes sell their time and ability in order to accomplish professional tasks.

The key difference is that they work for employers. They receive a salary determined by their employers, in other words, they are on the buyer’s market. Employers buy (employ) workers and pay them no more than the average salary by market standards. This salary is only enough to provide the basic necessities in the Slave caste. The slaves are dependent on the availability of work and a consistent paycheck. If their consistent paycheck disappears, then there is a high certainty that their condition will worsen substantially. Because slaves have insignificant income, it is hard for them to create reserves, and therefore be independent.

**INCOME IN THE MIDDLE CASTES**

The middle castes, or the professional castes, sell not only their time and ability to accomplish professional tasks, but also their reputation. Their reputation creates additional worth, which allows them to tack on an additional price to the cost of their work. In addition, professionals work in the seller’s market and have the opportunity to choose their own buyers, projects and employers. A higher income allows them to improve their quality of life, hire helpers, and accrue financial reserves. In this way, the level of independence in the middle castes increases substantially.

Nonetheless, any activity in the middle castes has one major setback: your income depends on you working daily. If those in the middle class stop working, they stop receiving income.

This is a serious limitation. You cannot earn more money than you can work for. No matter how much of a specialist you are and how much you charge, you only have 24 hours in a day. Nevertheless, those in the higher-middle castes can earn honorary titles, which put them in the rentier caste.

**INCOME IN THE HIGHER CASTES**

We now come to the key characteristics of the higher castes. The higher castes not only work for themselves, but give work to others. They have people working under them and earning money for them. This is a large leap in the level of opportunity available.

Your income is unlimited if you can employ an unlimited number of people.

The only limitation to your income is the level of demand for your product or service. A significant addition in the higher castes is the ability to receive passive income on which you can capitalize. Your income exceeds your expenditures to the point that you can not only accrue savings, but can also make investments and further increase your income. With enough money from investments, you can increase your income from your main line of work as well.

As a result, those in the higher castes have under their control a system of acquiring and increasing income. Their income is no longer dependent on their personal efforts. The higher castes can do whatever they’d like and they’ll still have an increasing level of income and opportunity.

* Those in the lower castes work, but all their efforts go only towards maintaining their own sad existence. The amount opportunities available to them will never grow.
* Those in the middle castes maintain a higher living standard, but their opportunities are limited by the time and effort they put in to their work.
* Those in the higher castes enjoy an exceptional standard of life and have unlimited opportunity for growth, and the rate by which their opportunities grow is also increasing.

This is the most important thesis: if you want to achieve something in life, you must move to the higher castes. **Only there will the opportunities available to you grow, and only there will you stop being limited by your own time constraints.**

**THE MEANING OF ROLES IN THE GROWTH SCHEME**

The growth strategy, within the framework of the caste metaphor, is a movement from the Slave caste, which is characterized by the fact that you must spend your own time, to the higher castes, where you can create a system that brings you income, independent on whether or not you spend your time.

In essence, you must first increase your income, then become independent from employers and clients, and then create a system where other people work for you, bringing in income, freeing you even more from your work, and putting you in a position of power. Your concrete strategy depends on the caste and dominating role you occupy now.

The main thesis of the Theory of Castes and Roles says that movement to the higher castes is based on changing roles from a less effective one to a more effective one. The less effective roles are those which don’t have the potential to increase your income and give you more freedom to develop.

The executor role has minimal potential to increase income and social status, so the executor does only the work assigned to him. He works in the material realm. This role is occupied by all workers, proletariats, peasants, service workers, low-level medical personnel and hundreds of other workers in various specializations. I won’t elaborate further on which social positions they hold. I also hope that among the readership of this book there is a minimal number of people for whom this role is dominant.

Let me clarify a little: your role is not equivalent to the current work or position you hold.

**Your role is the totality of your dominating stereotypes, determined by your value system, priorities, interactions with people and situations, connected to increasing your income and developing your creative potential.**

If your dominating role is as an executor, this means that you and the value of your work have minimal potential for growth. You honestly love what you do: working with machines, cutting peoples’ hair, selling flowers, building houses, baking, cleaning sewer systems and doing a million other things. If you want to occupy this role, then you have no chance for growth, because your work is the simplest work on the planet—artists work with subjects, not with people. How nice it would be to only interact with your craft or work and still experience financial and social growth. The exception is jewelers and those who create unique pieces. For that reason, the Executor role does not give you what you need to develop and move to the higher castes. In agrarian, archaic and developing countries a vast number of people are in the slave castes and have Executor as their dominant role. True, there is a way to overcome these limitations. We’ll talk about that later.

In more or less developed industrial and post-industrial countries, a large number of people have Specialist of Educator as their dominant role. This is to be expected, as the economy demands a large number of qualified people doing intellectual labor and there is a high demand for professional knowledge and skills. That’s why there’s a great number of teachers, trainers, coaches and consultants. These days you can find a specialist for even the most unusual problems. Since there is a large demand for information about specialists and accompanying information, there is an equally large number of agents who supply an intermediary service, finding necessary information, closing deals, and making relationships with legal and non-legal entities.

The business of Specialists, Educators and Agents is to sell their own time, qualifications, information and prepared solutions.

People whose personalities are dominated by these roles work in private and governmental institutions on all different levels. Employers set a task in front of them and they complete it within the framework of their own skill set and level of aptitude. They research, search for, create, analyze, enumerate, and devise solutions, while keeping a record of their work. Those occupying these roles perform a million different intellectual tasks tied to a million different abstract concepts, but they, as a rule, play no part in managing other people. This being the case, they have virtually no chance of moving to the higher castes because, if you remember, we live in a world occupied by people, and our progress in this world is dependent on how we interact with others, and not on how we interact with material objects or abstract concepts.

Why do those for which these roles are dominant have no chance for growth or progress? Because growth implies either improving your social status, increasing your income, or moving up caste levels, meaning reaching the Professional, Free Agent or Entrepreneurial castes. This demands a completely different set of personality traits, which these roles lack.

When you find a job, you become part of a hierarchy, and your position within the hierarchy is defined by two parameters: your position and area of specialization. The higher your status, the higher your income and the higher the caste you occupy, even if you work for hire. If you’re working for someone else, you can have one of three different statuses, which correspond to three different castes. They are as following:

* You can be in the Slave caste and hold the role of Executor, Specialist, Educator, Trader or Manager;
* You can be in the Top Manager or Security Official Castes and hold the role of Entrepreneur, Financier, Manager or Politician.

As I already stated, the main values of people with these roles is practicing their own craft, which does not allow them to grow their income or social status (position.) You may ask, how is this so?

The roles of Specialist and Educator do not have the most important qualities necessary to increase your income or social status, qualities which become more prevalent in all roles higher than Trader. From this we can draw a fundamental conclusion, which we already discussed in the first chapter.

You need professional skills in order to competently complete the tasks laid out in front of you, but that alone is not enough to increase your income level or social status. The reason for this, is that your income and social status are still dependent on another person, the other people participating in the deal, your boss, or some other influential figure. Whether or not you earn more money or improve your position is influenced only by how you interact with and build a relationship with your employer. Your position, in other words, is dependent on whether or not you have at least a minimal level of social skill and experience managing other people. These are the skills that you don’t see among Specialists and Educators, because their specific value system is based on reaching the higher levels of their profession, finding truth, and other such conventions.

Specialists don’t understand that. Any encouragement in the form of a pay or status raise is an evaluation. You’re being evaluated. And people are evaluating you. This myth is only half true.

Remember: if you have a good relationship with someone, they’ll forgive the mistakes you make, but if you have a bad relationship, they’ll give you no help whatsoever.

Specialists and Educators who are able to do their jobs well often are not in the position to build productive relationships with their superiors and colleagues, because their system of values creates competition. Many Specialists seem to think that if their boss doesn’t have a good professional skill set, then they are worse than the specialist himself. To some extent, Specialists are all obsessed with some idea around which all of their professional activity revolves and without which they could not survive. While a deal is being made, the buyer wants to lower the price. Theoretically, the Specialist, selling his own time and effort, needs to raise the price, although in doing so, he risks losing work. In this situation, the Specialist often chooses to work for a lower price and gives up the opportunity to make more money. In order to build the foundation for increasing your status and moving to the higher castes, you must add the role of Trader to your arsenal, as it balances out the roles of Specialist and Educator.

In order to build the foundation for increasing your status and opportunity to moving to the higher castes, you must add the role of Trader to your arsenal.

The Trader’s goal is to make deals which are as beneficial to him as possible. The Trader conducts an enormous number of human and financial transactions with the goal of creating better conditions for buying and selling. The Trader’s formula for success is buying what is guaranteed to be in demand for the lowest price possible. This is a law many people do not understand. **You don’t cash in on your sales, you cash in on your purchases.** Amateurs say, “If you want to improve your material position, then sell.” This is true, in part. The whole equation looks like this: First, find something that is good to sell, then buy it at the lowest market price.

Inexperienced sellers bargain with the buyers. Experienced sellers bargain with the sellers.

If you buy a product at market price, then you’ll have to try much harder to convince your buyer to even buy it back at the same price. If you buy at a lower-than-market price, then you don’t need to convince your buyer. His own greed does the work for you, because you can sell it back to him at a lower-than-market price. All these principles are in play when you are presenting a product.

FREE WILL AND KARMA**

**DESIRE, MEANING AND VALUE**

People face many problems associated with desire and meaning in life. These problems arise because people don’t understand:

* what they want;
* what they can want;
* what has meaning

Since the idea of meaning or purpose in life is often over-dramatized, most people, instead of really putting any thought into it, simply twiddle their thumbs. You see, if you don’t have goals or meaning, then you won’t go anywhere and you’ll be swept away by by life’s current. You have no idea where it will take you. If you have a choice, however, between being buffeted around on the winds of chance or to move in a purposeful and predetermined direction then, in my point of view, it’s better to choose the path that allows you to control your own destiny.

* illness;
* poverty and loss;
* enemies and bad relationships with people;
* various other problems;
* limited freedom.

If you have all of the things on the first list, and avoid all those on the second, then you reach a feeling of harmony.

**THE PRINCIPLES OF LIFE: INTERNAL BALANCE**

Besides values—the things that we want in life—there are also principles. Principles are reasonable limitations to the way be obtain the things we value, so that in the process of obtaining them, we don’t bring harm to ourselves or those around us. Principles define what is “environmentally friendly” for our internal systems, and what is not. Any violation of your internal balance comes from ignoring important principles, which leads to negative long-term consequences, and therefore to the likelihood of losing some of your valued materials. If you acquire things through theft or burglary, it is likely that they will be taken back from you and limits will be placed on your freedom.

Some people, and they are in the majority, value the idea of not creating problems for people around them. Many of us are simply taught to do unto others, what you would have done unto you. This is a moral limitation that forbids the use of some seemingly effective methods, due to the fact that they bring harm to others.

A person who has moral limitations is more likely to accept the impossibility of acquiring a valued item than to create problems for other people by acquiring it. The negative feelings you experience from a sense of guilt outweigh the negative feelings you experience from poverty or loss. Because of this, people are constantly faced with a choice: which of these experiences they would rather avoid. If fear of loss overpowers moral principles, then a person will violate these principles, and if the threat of losing your reputation or of guilt is stronger, then he won’t.

These moral principles and limitations to our action are a fundamental part of life. We consciously act on them, in order to have harmony in the long run. Some people violate these principles and pursue values in a more aggressive way, but in time this will catch up to them. If you achieving happiness at others’ expense, you will always eventually find yourself surrounded by a certain type of people, who will create problems for you.

Understanding these principles gives you the chance to reach your goals and gain meaning in a more balanced way. Besides that, you won’t feel the pangs of conscience and accompanying doubt. This is important, since according to Maslow’s hierarchy of needs, when a person fulfils his basic needs, he can turn his sights to fulfilling desires and higher needs, such as social realization or recognition. Your efforts will be recognized, as long as they don’t cause other people loss or trouble. Otherwise people will be suspicious of, and even despise you, and the likelihood of a mutiny or other unconstructive behavior increases. And there is always the risk of losing the benefits you gained by being willing to slight others and endure their animosity. This is not due to some sort of cosmic justice, it’s far more pragmatic: someone, sooner or later, will simply lose patience with you.

**THE UNIVERSAL THEORY OF CASTES AND ROLES: LOOKING DEEPER**

The most common myths will tell you that there are many paths to walk in this world. This is an illusion: many of the paths are only available to those who are the masters of their own fates, but for those who aren’t, there is only one path: slavery.

This means, that if you take a careful look at your fate on the caste and role matrix, then you’ll understand, that externally oriented people have access to a million different fates, but if you really get to the core of each of them, using the caste and role system, then there are really only two kinds.

**There are successful paths and unsuccessful ones. That’s it.**

Successful fates are built by following the proper paths through the Specialist, Trader, Manager, Entrepreneur, Financier and Politician roles.

The unsuccessful fates come to be when you only hold one or two roles: Specialist, or Specialist and Trader, or Trader and Entrepreneur. Any partial combination of the fundamental roles leads to an unsuccessful fate, even if you have a vision or are a very intelligent person.

And all of this is because the fundamental roles are directed towards relationships with themes and objects, and **the rest depends on your relationships with other people.**
